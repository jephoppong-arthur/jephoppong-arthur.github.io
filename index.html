About Me

Welcome! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the School of Economic Sciences, Washington State University. I hold an M.S. in Statistics and an M.A. in Economics from the University of Akron.

My research integrates economic theory, applied econometrics, artificial intelligence, and mathematical optimization to address challenges in energy markets and the transition toward sustainable, efficient, and resilient energy systems.

Research Interests

Energy Economics, Environmental Economics, Applied Econometrics, Computational Economics, and Data Science.

Job Market Paper

Designing a Self-sufficient Community Microgrid: An AI-Agent-Based Energy Trading Framework with Integrated Storage

This research develops an autonomous agent-based electricity trading framework that integrates distributed renewable generation, battery storage optimization, and decentralized household decision-making to promote economically efficient and resilient community microgrids.
