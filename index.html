About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at Washington State University, currently on the academic job market. My research integrates microeconomic theory, applied econometrics, artificial intelligence, and mathematical optimization to address challenges in energy markets and the sustainable energy transition.

Research Interests

Research Fields: Energy Economics, Financial Economics, and Applied Industrial Organization.

Research Methods: Applied Econometrics and Computational Economics.

Research

Job Market Paper

Designing a Self-sufficient Community Microgrid: An AI-Agent-Based Energy Trading Framework with Integrated Storage

This research develops a decentralized electricity trading framework for community microgrids that integrates distributed solar generation, battery energy storage, household optimization, and iterative auction-based price discovery.

The framework examines how coordinated local electricity trading and energy storage can improve economic efficiency, support renewable energy integration, and strengthen community energy resilience.

Research in Progress

Data Centers and Electricity Prices

This project investigates the effects of data center development on electricity prices, using applied econometric methods to examine how growing electricity demand affects local energy markets.

The Effect of Temperature on Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure and Adoption

This research examines the relationship between temperature, electric vehicle charging infrastructure, and electric vehicle adoption.
